### Overview
Zotero OCR integrates Tesseract and Poppler utilities to perform OCR directly on scanned PDF attachments, making text searchable and selectable.

### Packaging Details
- Packaged as a system-wide Zotero extension.
- Works best when `poppler` and `tesseract` are installed.
